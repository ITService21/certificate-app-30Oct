## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-assurance maritime compliance verification interface representing Continental Training Center S.A. (CTC) and United Alliance Group Ltd, Panama. The visual language conveys institutional legitimacy, legal sovereignty, and rapid operational clarity for port authorities, shipowners, vetting inspectors, and seafarers worldwide.

Drawing from **Corporate / Modern** precision combined with archival security document ergonomics, the interface evokes the gravitas of maritime credentialing:
- **Authority & Integrity:** Unambiguous authenticity indicators, cryptographically signed record representations, and formal maritime seal iconography.
- **High-Stakes Legibility:** High-contrast data architecture engineered for inspection in low-light ship bridges, sunlit open decks, and port control terminals.
- **Official Status Hierarchy:** Prominent, instant-verdict state containers (Valid, Expiring Soon, Revoked/Suspended) eliminating ambiguity during port state control audits.

## Colors

The palette leverages deep nautical blues balanced with dynamic verification states and clean canvas backgrounds across both light and dark operational modes.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Blue (`#0A7AC1`):** Action targets, active tab strokes, interactive certificate identifiers, and verifiable document links.
- **Deep Maritime Navy (`#0B1F4B`):** Structural authority, header bands, institutional seals, security watermarks, and high-hierarchy headings in light mode.
- **Background Light (`#F8FAFC`):** Crisp archival surface for high-readability daylight verification.
- **Background Dark (`#0B1220`):** Deep navy-black night-watch surface reducing glare for nocturnal bridge and inspection use.

### Status Indicators
- **Valid (`#10B981`):** Verified authentic STCW credential, active certification, compliant compliance record.
- **Expiring Soon (`#F59E0B`):** Renewal threshold reached (within 90 days of expiration), cautionary operational note.
- **Revoked / Expired (`#EF4444`):** Lapsed validity, administrative revocation, flagged or invalid checksum.

### Dual-Mode Surface Mapping
In light mode, cards utilize `#FFFFFF` with precise 1px borders of `#E2E8F0`. In dark mode, card surfaces transition to `#131F37`, elevated surfaces to `#1E2E4F`, and borders to `#2A3F68`.

## Typography

Typography relies on a systematic deployment of **Inter** across all functional tiers, prioritizing extreme geometric clarity and high legibility.

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum` / `cv01`):** All certificate numbers, passport IDs, resolution dates, and IMO regulation identifiers must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` to ensure vertical column alignment across dense security audit tables.
- **Official Nomenclature:** Seafarer names, certification titles, and governing treaty bodies (e.g., *STCW 1978, as amended*) utilize semibold to extra-bold weights with uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) to mirror printed physical parchment credentials.
- **Hierarchy Distinction:** Clear visual separation between institutional headers, candidate metadata, legal resolution citations, and cryptographic verification stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to display multi-attribute maritime credentials, side-by-side verification seals, and security QR modules.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** Max-width containment at `1280px` centered. 12 columns with 32px (`2rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) page margins. Splits into a credential validation dossier on the primary 8 columns and security metadata (QR code, digital signature status, authority seals) on the remaining 4 columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with 24px gutters and 24px margins. Metadata folds below the primary verification certificate card.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns with 16px gutters and 16px margins. Linear flow where the instant status badge anchors the top viewport, followed immediately by seafarer identity, course authorization, and QR proof.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Strict multiples of 4px and 8px govern component padding to retain alignment with international compliance software standards.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids exaggerated, decorative drop shadows in favor of a restrained, high-legibility documentary aesthetic:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base canvas (`#F8FAFC` light / `#0B1220` dark)
  - Primary document surface (`#FFFFFF` light / `#131F37` dark)
  - Inspection panels & sub-cards (`#F1F5F9` light / `#1E2E4F` dark)
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Surfaces are bounded by crisp 1px borders (`#CBD5E1` in light, `#2A3F68` in dark).
- **Audit Elevation (Float & Focus):** Interactive inspection modals, search autocompletes, and popover certificate previews employ a subtle, maritime-tinted ambient shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(11, 31, 75, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 31, 75, 0.04)` (Light mode)
  - `box-shadow: 0 6px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45), 0 0 0 1px rgba(42, 63, 104, 0.8)` (Dark mode)
- **Security Watermark Layer:** Translucent vector seals (`opacity: 0.04` in light mode, `opacity: 0.06` in dark mode) sit between card background and textual data to visually reinforce authenticity without impairing OCR scanners.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft (`1`)** shape language (`4px` / `0.25rem` standard border-radius, `8px` / `0.5rem` for major cards and modals).

- **Intent:** Sharper corners and minimal curvature mirror traditional sovereign documentation, security ledgers, and maritime logbooks. Rounded elements are strictly reserved for status indicators.
- **Badges & Chips:** Compact `4px` corner radii for metadata tags; full pill rounding (`9999px`) is applied exclusively to the primary verification outcome pill (e.g., `VALID / VERIFIED`) to provide an instant organic visual contrast against the surrounding linear data fields.
- **Document Containers:** Outer certificate cards carry `8px` radius with dual-stroke security border accents matching national maritime authority aesthetics.

## Components

### Verification Status Banner
- **Valid Banner:** Emerald green fill (`#ECFDF5` light / `#064E3B` dark), 1px solid green border (`#10B981`), featuring an encrypted checkmark glyph, bold uppercase status `OFFICIALLY VERIFIED & VALID`, and live UTC timestamp.
- **Expiring / Revoked Banners:** Equivalent high-visibility containers utilizing Amber (`#FEF3C7` / `#78350F`) and Red (`#FEF2F2` / `#7F1D1D`) surface pairings.

### Primary Verification Card
- **Structure:** Dual top header featuring CTC and United Alliance Group Ltd emblems, certificate tracking ID (`UAG-TC-XX-XXXXX`), and official resolution bar.
- **Data Pairs:** Stacked label-value layout with `label-sm` in muted slate (`#64748B` light / `#94A3B8` dark) and values in `body-md` bold tabular numbers.
- **Authority Endorsement Area:** Signature block with digitized signature display, officer title (`CTC General Manager`), and embedded verification hash code.

### Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Search Button:** Deep Maritime Navy background (`#0B1F4B`), crisp white text, hover transition to `#0A7AC1`, 4px radius, uppercase tracking.
- **Input Fields:** 42px height, 1px border (`#94A3B8`), crisp tabular text input, inline validation badge, and distinct focus ring with `#0A7AC1` glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(10, 122, 193, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary Actions:** Ghost buttons with 1px border matching container stroke and icon prefixes (e.g., "Download Certified PDF", "Print Verification Transcript").

### Security Seal & QR Code Block
- **QR Enclosure:** Clean 128x128px high-contrast QR matrix bordered by subtle navigational corner brackets, with human-readable tracking string underneath.
- **Seal Ornament:** Circular vector badge of Panama/UAG maritime anchor motif with fine concentric micro-text border rings.

### Dark/Light Mode Switcher
- Compact header control with sun/moon iconography, 4px rounded pill structure, and high-contrast toggle state ensuring instant compliance with marine night vision protocols.
